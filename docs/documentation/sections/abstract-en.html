Abstract

PROTO models proverbs, the tacit (unspoken) lessons they carry, the situations in which they are used, and the cross-cultural equivalences that link a proverb in one language to a proverb in another when they teach the same lesson but diverge (or don't) in imagery. The current dataset covers 28 proverbs across four languages (English, Farsi, Italian, and Albanian), grounded in Wikidata for their tacit lessons and source-domain imagery.
