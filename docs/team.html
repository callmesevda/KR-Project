Who built this

Team

PROTO is a four-person project: ontology modeling, cross-cultural analysis, native-language verification, and dissemination.

SR

Sevda Rezaei Melal

Knowledge graph (ABox)

Built the PROTO knowledge graph — populating instance data for proverbs, source domains and cross-cultural equivalence links — and published it live on TriplyDB with a public SPARQL endpoint.

PH

Polikseni Hasanaj

Cross-cultural analysis

Reviewed all 48 cross-cultural equivalence links by hand against the original text, translation and situation of use — confirming, correcting, and proposing PartialOverlap and FalseFriend cases the automatic classification can't see. See the analyst review.

SA

Saba Afsharzadehtorghab

Persian-language verification

Native-speaker consultant confirming the Persian proverb readings and candidate additions identified during the cross-cultural review.

CB

Claudia Briccolani

Ontology structure model (TBox) & dissemination

Designed the PROTO class and property structure, including the CrossCulturalEquivalence reification and the DirectMatch equivalence type. Also built this website, generated the WIDOCO documentation, and put together the defense slides.

Polikseni Hasanaj appears as "Ksenia" in the analyst review document.
